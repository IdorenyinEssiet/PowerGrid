# PowerGrid - Energy Grid Management System

A blockchain-based platform for managing renewable energy distribution, tracking production, and rewarding efficient energy providers.

## Features
- Energy production tracking
- Grid coordinator management
- Efficiency assessment system
- Premium distribution for clean energy

## Smart Contract Functions
- Record energy production
- Assess grid efficiency
- Distribute efficiency premiums
- Track energy statistics

## Usage
Enables transparent renewable energy grid management with blockchain verification.
